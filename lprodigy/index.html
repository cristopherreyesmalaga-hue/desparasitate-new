Countertops

Choose your stone

Marble

One of the most popular and elegant surfaces for years. A natural material that gives your kitchen a sophisticated, exclusive touch.

Granite

One of the strongest and most attractive stones produced by nature — a valuable choice inside and out, with unmatched elegance and quality.

Quartzite

Stronger than granite, with the look of exotic marble. Its hardness keeps it beautiful for years, free of scratches and etching.

Quartz

90% quartz mineral and 10% resin: stain-resistant, crack-resistant and maintenance-free. Jumbo slabs (127×64) include Calacatta Bergamo and Calacatta Girgi.

Porcelain

Made from China clay rich in kaolinite, silica and feldspar, which give it exceptional strength — for elegant, long-lasting surfaces.

Semi-precious stones

Quartz, amethyst and natural agate, prized since ancient times — used to design surfaces that turn a kitchen into a statement.
